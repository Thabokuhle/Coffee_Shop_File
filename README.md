# 🏪 Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

This repository presents insights and analysis based on sales performance data of a coffee shop, focusing on product category revenue, time-based trends, and store location performance.

---

## 📊 Key Analyses

### 1️⃣ Revenue per Product Category at Different Times of the Day
- Coffee generates the highest revenue overall  
- Morning and afternoon are the peak times for coffee sales  
- Coffee attracts commuters and break-time customers  

### 2️⃣ Total Revenue per Product Category
- **Top performers:** Coffee & Tea  
- **Low performers:** Packaged chocolate & flavors  
- Coffee generates almost **4× more revenue** than drinking chocolate  

### 3️⃣ Top 10 Selling Products per Day
- Morning drives the highest sales volumes  
- Barista Coffee & Brewed Herbal Tea are top sellers  
- Scones are popular in the morning  
- Evening revenue is lowest, with brewed coffee preferred  

### 4️⃣ Total Product Revenue by Store Location
- **Lower Manhattan** consistently generates the highest revenue  
- Similar trends observed across hot beverage categories  

---

## ✅ Key Insights Summary

| Insight Category        | Summary |
|------------------------|--------|
| **Top Product**        | Coffee is the highest-earning product |
| **Peak Sales Time**    | Morning & Afternoon |
| **Customer Preference**| Hot beverages drive most sales |
| **Low Performing Items** | Packaged chocolate & flavor products |
| **Best Location**      | Lower Manhattan stores |

---

## 📌 Recommendations

- Increase coffee & tea supply during morning/afternoon rush  
- Promote chocolate & flavor categories to boost sales  
- Offer bundle deals (example: **coffee + pastry**)  
- Allocate more staff & inventory to Lower Manhattan branches  
- Introduce loyalty rewards for morning commuters
